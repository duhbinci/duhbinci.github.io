## 3D printing (shell + shelf + lid)

The three parts are about 710 cm³ of solid plastic, so roughly **700 – 880 g of PLA** depending on slicer settings (3 mm walls print almost solid). One 1 kg spool covers it.

| Option | NPR | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Your own printer, 1 kg PLA spool | ~2,800 | **confirmed Rs 2,800** on [Daraz](https://www.daraz.com.np/products/pla-filament-1kg-175mm-i105727196.html) (out of stock when checked) |
| Print service, Robotics Association of Nepal | 28,000 – 35,200 | their published rate: Rs 50/g, Rs 40/g over 100 g ([ran.org.np](https://www.ran.org.np/3d-printing-services/)); build volume 30 × 30 × 30 cm fits every part |

PETG instead of PLA is worth considering for the shell: the cooler's exhaust air runs warm and PETG tolerates heat better. Expect a similar spool price (estimate).

## Totals

| Scenario | NPR |
|---|---|
| Parts + your own filament | **~10,300 – 16,900** |
| Parts + print service | **~35,500 – 49,300** |

Not included: the hard drives themselves, and anything for BIOS display (a spare 15.6" eDP panel or an eDP-to-DP adapter, only if the free HDMI test fails).

At the print-service rate the printing costs more than all the parts together. A local makerspace or a friend's printer is worth asking; most shops quote lower on large, simple prints if you ask for a per-hour or bulk price.
